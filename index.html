This is the live companion to the lecture. The ten interactive demos below are numbered in the order the lecturer will visit them. When the slide says Demo N, click card Demo N.

Each card opens in a new tab, so you can return here between demos. No installation, no login. Each demo runs entirely in your browser.

The ten demos

Demo 1 / 10 Act I · Compression
Block-diagonal puzzle
Drag rows and columns of a scrambled rating matrix until four clean blocks emerge. Compression by hand.

Demo 2 / 10 Act I · Compression
MovieLens block-diagonal
The same puzzle on real data: forty users and twenty-eight movies from the public MovieLens 100K. The toy was rank-2 by construction; this is genuine ratings, and the blocks are still there.
open in new tab →
Demo 3 / 10 Act I · Compression
Spectral microscope
SVD as a slider. Watch the matrix sharpen as k grows from 1; the elbow at k=2 is Eckart-Young.
open in new tab →
Demo 4 / 10 Act I · Compression
Embeddings that learn
Random embeddings, gradient descent. Watch the four-corner geometry self-organise. Re-seed and watch it again.
open in new tab →
Demo 5 / 10 Interlude · Shannon
Entropy mountain
Drag an urn's bias from 0 to 1 and trace H(p). The compression floor, made physical.
open in new tab →
Demo 6 / 10 Act II · Prediction
Three-urn casino
Three campaigns, three hidden conversion rates. Play 30 rounds, then watch ε-greedy and naive replay your seed.
open in new tab →
Demo 7 / 10 Act II · Prediction
ε-greedy walkthrough
One click per micro-step. The roulette spins, the branch decides exploit or explore, a ball is pulled, the estimate updates. Watch the algorithm one step at a time.
open in new tab →
Demo 8 / 10 Act II · Prediction
Algorithm stadium
Same urns, same rounds. Five strategies racing on cumulative regret — with a slider for the explore parameter.
open in new tab →
Demo 9 / 10 Act II · Prediction
Distinguishability dial
Two urns, slider for the gap. Sample size explodes quadratically as the urns approach each other.
open in new tab →
Demo 10 / 10 Epilogue · Duality
Compression-prediction twin
A forecaster on the left, an arithmetic encoder on the right. Three modes: binary urn, English text, character-level Markov LM. Loss = rate, bit-exact.
