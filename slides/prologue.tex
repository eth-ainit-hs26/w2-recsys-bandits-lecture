% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
% Scene 2c --- Reframe: encoder = compression, decoder = prediction
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Two halves of one machine}
  The encoder and decoder have older names.

  \medskip
  \begin{itemize}
    \setlength{\itemsep}{0.5em}
    \item Encoder \(\approx\) \AINITemph{compression}.
          A long sequence becomes a short vector.
    \item Decoder \(\approx\) \AINITemph{prediction}.
          The vector becomes a bet about what comes next.
  \end{itemize}

  \medskip
  This framework is the core of many modern machine learning
  technologies, and originates from a 1948 paper by Shannon.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{The promise}
  Machine learning is the engineering of two old theorems.
  \AINITemph{Compression}, the shortest faithful description of
  the past, and \AINITemph{prediction}, the best bet about the future,
  are two views of the same subject, governed by Shannon's 1948 theorems.
  We will build them in two halves and collapse them in the third.
\end{frame}
