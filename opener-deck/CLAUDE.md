## Gotchas

- **Every frame is drawn in mm** (`x=1mm, y=1mm`, bounding box `(0,1) rectangle (130,-54)`). The
  kit's `\sheetgrid` and `\sheetband` add offsets of 0.15, 0.28 and 0.41 that only make sense in cm,
  so use them only in a picture in cm.
- **To change a number, change `tools/opener_numbers.py`** and rerun it. Bar lengths come from it as
  macros (`\barMetaApp`, `\barNuIncome`, `\barAmzWith`), so a new value moves its bar too.
- **`tools/check_numbers.py` reads `pdftotext -layout`**, where a gloss beside the bars shares its
  lines with them. A phrase that wraps inside a gloss is cut by the bar labels, so expect only a
  number and its unit ("100 million"), and tie them with `~` in the source.
- **A gloss is 13.8 mm wider than its text width** (inner xsep 6.9 mm on each side). At x = 84 the
  widest text that stays inside the 130 mm box is 3.2 cm.
- **Frames 4 and 5 quote earnings calls, and frame 6 a paper with five results**. The slides say "no
  method given" for the calls; the Say comments carry what the slides leave out (Meta's ad revenue,
  Nubank's second market). Keep conversions apart from revenue when editing frame 4.
- **This repository is public and every push to main publishes it on GitHub Pages**, the deck included.
