# CLAUDE.md : opener-deck/, the opener of weekend 2

Six frames (21 pages with the clicks) that open weekend 2 of AINIT HS26, 13 and 14 November 2026: why
recommendation, advertising and customer profiles matter, told to managers. Built on 2026-11-03 at
Carlos's request ("Build it in 6 slides, please") in the Codex plates style of `../style/`. README.md
has the frames, the sources with their grades, the choices made without asking, the simplifications
and the open items. The research behind it is in
`/Users/carloscotrini/Documents/git_ainit/research_notes/Business value of recommendation and profiling/`
(a local folder outside this repository).

## Gotchas

- **Frames 1 to 3 are drawn in mm, frames 4 to 6 in cm.** `\adbar` and `\sharebox` assume a picture
  in mm; `\sheetgrid` and `\sheetband` add offsets of 0.15, 0.28 and 0.41 that only make sense in cm.
- **To change a number, change `tools/opener_numbers.py`** and rerun it. Its asserts encode the story
  of frame 4 (calling by risk picks Lea and Marco and raises the chance of leaving, calling by
  response picks Nina and Jonas and lowers it), so a new table that breaks the story fails loudly.
  Say "more likely to cancel" for Marco: his chance goes from 70% to 75%, which is no certainty.
- **`chip strong` needs room for its hatched frame**: frames 5 and 6 pass
  `inner xsep=2.6mm, inner ysep=2.4mm` (2.2mm on frame 6). Without it the text runs over the hatching.
- **Frame 5's number is the AMA's, and the slide says so.** The paper's abstract (Crossref) gives
  "two field experiments" and no number; the 4.1 to 8.7 points, the Middle East and "in some
  instances" more churn come from the AMA's award release of 2023, and "simulations" from its release
  of 2019. An earlier talk abstract says "up to seven percentage points". Once someone reads the
  paper through the ETH library (README.md, open item 3), credit the paper alone.
- **This repository is public and every push to main publishes it on GitHub Pages**, the deck included.
