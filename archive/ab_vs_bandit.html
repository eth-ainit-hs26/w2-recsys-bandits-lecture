Why this matters

A/B testing wastes traffic on losers. The bandit reallocates as it learns. The lift you see above is the dollar value of that reallocation — pure revenue recovered from clicks that would otherwise have been spent gathering evidence we already had.

The decision rule is upper-confidence bound: at each step, pick $\arg\max_k \big(\hat{\mu}_k + \sqrt{2\ln t / n_k}\big)$. Both simulators draw from the same hidden CTRs and the same RNG sequence, so the only difference you see is the allocation policy itself.
