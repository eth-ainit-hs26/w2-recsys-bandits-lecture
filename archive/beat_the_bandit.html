Beat the Bandit

Three slot machines. Hidden win-rates. Fifty rounds. UCB and Thompson sampling are playing the same machines beside you, on independent draws. Can you out-decide them?

Setup

Each round, all three players (you, UCB, Thompson) pick an arm. The bandit then draws a separate Bernoulli reward for each player from the same hidden mean. So the comparison is policy vs. policy — luck cancels in expectation, the policy gap doesn't.

What they're computing
UCB1 picks
$\arg\max_i \left(\hat{\mu}_i + \sqrt{\frac{2 \ln t}{n_i}}\right)$
Thompson picks
$\theta_i \sim \text{Beta}(\alpha_i, \beta_i),\ \arg\max_i \theta_i$

UCB adds an exploration bonus that shrinks as an arm gets pulled. Thompson keeps a Beta belief per arm and picks by sampling. Both have perfect memory of their own history.
