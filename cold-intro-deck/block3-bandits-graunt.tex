% ===== 1. The setting =====
% Say: The weekly Bills of Mortality, printed by London's parish clerks: the burials of each parish and the causes of
% death. Graunt tells how they were made (chapter 1): when someone died, the searchers, "antient Matrons, sworn to
% their office", viewed the body and reported the disease or casualty to the parish clerk; every Tuesday night the
% clerk carried the burials and christenings of the week to the clerk of the Hall; on Wednesday the bill was made up
% and printed, and on Thursday it was published, for four shillings a year.
% The picture: the bill of 5 to 12 September 1665, during the Great Plague: 7690 burials that week, 6544 of them of
% the plague.
