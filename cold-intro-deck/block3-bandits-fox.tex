% ===== 5. The moral =====
% Say: The moral of the fable. "From the evidence" is ek tekmerion in the Greek. Two later versions. Horace, Epistles

% Renard" (1668): "Je vois fort bien comme l'on entre, / Et ne vois pas comme on en sort": I see very well how one
% goes in, and I do not see how one comes out.
