What's happening?

Each urn has a hidden Bernoulli bias — the chance that a single draw is a "win". On every round, the audience votes which urn to sample; that decision uses only the running empirical means p̂ = wins / picks. After 30 rounds, click Reveal & Replay to expose the biases and re-run three algorithms — naive explore-then-exploit, ε-greedy, and UCB — on the same hidden draws. The cumulative-reward curves show how each strategy spent its 30 chances.
