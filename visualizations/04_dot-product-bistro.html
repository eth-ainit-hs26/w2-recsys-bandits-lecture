Tip

Drag any bar up or down. Same-sign pairs make the product green and add to the score; opposite signs make it red and subtract.

What's happening?

The score "how much will this user like this item?" is computed by lining up two vectors and multiplying matching coordinates, then adding everything together. That's it. No deep magic — just multiplying and adding numbers.

What makes those numbers taste is which features they encode. With Simple, Tech, Indoor, Outdoor as the 4 dimensions, a positive product means user and item agree on that axis. The sum is the recommendation score.
