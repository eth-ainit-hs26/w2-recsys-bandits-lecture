What's happening?

We pre-train MF on all 21 users (slide the slider only after the model is trained — about 1 second). Then we freeze the item vectors $V$ and ask: given the first $N$ revealed ratings of User B, what's the best $u_B$? With items fixed this is a closed-form ridge regression: $u_B = (V_\Omega^{\!\top} V_\Omega + \lambda I)^{-1} V_\Omega^{\!\top} r_\Omega$.

At $N=0$ we have no signal: $u_B$ is the origin and predictions are flat. At $N=16$, $u_B$ lands in its true quadrant and accuracy on held-out cells is high. The interesting region is $N=1\!\dots\!4$ — the cliff. Which item should we ask about first? That question is the bridge to the bandits half of W2.
