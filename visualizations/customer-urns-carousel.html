Customer urns: each reader is a Bernoulli

Each reader is an urn. Inside: 100 balls, where the fraction of blue balls equals their probability of finishing A History of Western Philosophy (Russell). Click an urn to draw one ball — does this reader finish the book?

What's happening?

We treat “will reader i finish Russell?” as a Bernoulli random variable with bias pi. Readers who liked similar demanding nonfiction in the past have pi ≈ 0.92; readers whose tastes diverge have ≈ 0.08; the unknowns sit at 0.5. Stage 1 lets you sample these urns one ball at a time; the running estimator p̂ = whites / n shrinks toward the true bias as you click.
