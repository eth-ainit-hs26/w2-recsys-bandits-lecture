Embedding interpolation

An embedding is a personality vector. Slide between two users — their tastes morph smoothly along the line between them.

What's happening?

Each user has an embedding u — here a 2D vector along the Simple↔Technology and Indoor↔Outdoor axes. We linearly interpolate u(t) = (1−t)·uA + t·uB as you drag the slider.

The predicted rating of an item with embedding v is the dot product u(t)·v: positive (green) when the user-vector points the same way as the item, negative (red) when opposite. Watch the entire row of preferences morph smoothly — that's why embeddings are so powerful: a single slider mixes whole personalities.
