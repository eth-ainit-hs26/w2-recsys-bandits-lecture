    What's happening?

    Each dot is one of 50 hand-picked movies, coloured by genre. The 2D position is its embedding produced by a tiny offline matrix-factorization-style routine on simulated user ratings. Notice how genres cluster without anyone telling the algorithm what a genre is — that's the point.

    In You mode, click anywhere to drop a synthetic user into latent space; we recommend the movies whose embeddings are closest to where you stood. Drag the marker and watch the top-10 list morph from sci-fi to romance to documentaries.

    Honest disclaimer: the dataset is a curated synthetic toy — 50 real movie titles, simulated ratings, embeddings produced by a small offline routine. It is not the real MovieLens dataset, but it behaves the same way pedagogically.
