What's happening?

Each row is a reader, each column a book, and each cell is a rating. Initially the order is scrambled and the matrix looks like noise. By rearranging rows and columns, you can group readers who agree and books they agree on — making clear blocks of blue (✅) and orange (❌) appear along the diagonal.

The score measures how well each cell's rating matches the sign expected at its position from the canonical clustering (Commuters · Literary · Practical · Scholars × Light Fiction · Demanding Fiction · Light Nonfiction · Demanding Nonfiction). Reaching 85% means you've discovered (close to) the same clustering an embedding would find — except you did it by eye.
