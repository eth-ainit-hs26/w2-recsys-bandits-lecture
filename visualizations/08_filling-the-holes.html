What's happening?

We hide ~10% of known ratings (purple border) so we can grade ourselves, then train MF on what's left. As epochs pass, predictions for the unknown ? cells fade in (✅ / ➖ / ❌), while the validation MAE keeps the model honest.

Hover any cell to see the raw prediction $u_i^\top v_j$ — note the magnitude is a kind of confidence: predictions near 0 mean "the model isn't sure".
