    What's happening?

    Every item has a fixed position in the 2D latent plane. The user is a vector u from the origin. The score for an item at point p is the inner product u · p = u₁p₁ + u₂p₂ — positive (green) when u and p point the same way, negative (red) when they oppose.

    Rotating u rotates a green half-plane. Items inside it become recommendations; items behind it are suppressed. The same items, ranked differently, depending only on the user's direction.
