Movie clusters in the reveal

    What you are seeing

    Forty top-rated MovieLens users and twenty-eight movies, hand-curated across four genres (Family, Action, Drama, Comedy). Each cell is a real rating, mean-centred per user: a colour shows whether the user liked the film relative to their own usual, not whether they rated it high in absolute terms. (This strips out the “everyone rates high” bias of MovieLens.)

    In the scrambled view the matrix looks like noise. Click Reveal clusters and the same matrix is now sorted by k-means on the latent embeddings from a top-2 SVD. Like-coloured cells cluster on the diagonal: each user cluster's favourite movie cluster lights up.
