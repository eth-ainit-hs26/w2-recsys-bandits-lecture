What you're seeing

The colours. Each rating is compared with that person's own average. Blue means they liked the movie more than they usually like movies, orange less. That way someone who gives everything five stars doesn't look like a fan of everything.

The sorting. The computer does what you did by hand in Demo 1: it puts people who rated the movies alike next to each other, and movies rated alike next to each other inside each genre. Then it cuts the list of people into four groups where their tastes change most.

The averages. Each square shows how much one group liked one genre: 16 squares summarise 1,120 cells. Groups B, C and D each have a favourite genre (action, family and comedy), while Group A likes a bit of everything.
