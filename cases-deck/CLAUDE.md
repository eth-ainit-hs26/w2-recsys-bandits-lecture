# CLAUDE.md : cases-deck/, business cases of the weekend 2 techniques

A short talk for managers in four parts, one case at the end of each Friday lecture of weekend 2 of AINIT HS26
(13 November 2026), then a map: Etsy's search, nearest neighbours by inner product (08:00, FMLDS 2024);
Spotify's audiobooks, a vector per listener and per audiobook (10:00, WWW 2024); Adyen's payments, an
epsilon-greedy bandit (12:00, KDD workshop 2025); LinkedIn's ranking models, gradient descent at scale (14:00,
KDD 2024); then one frame that maps four business questions to the four techniques (14:00). Asked for on
2026-11-04 after the BMAI HS26 evaluation, whose most repeated request (six comments, and the same in HS25 and
FS26) was real business use cases. Carlos chose the slot ("one case per lecture") and the length (10 to 15
minutes in all) the same day. That evening he rejected version 1 (Airbnb 2018, the Netflix Prize, Netflix 2012;
commits `b840920` to `405acd3`) as old: "Results should be latest from 2021." It follows `../opener-deck/` and
repeats none of its numbers. README.md has the frames, the sources with their grades, the choices made without
asking and the open items.

Since 2026-11-06 the folder also holds one deck per case, each dictated by Carlos slide by slide: `etsy.tex` (5
frames: the shortlist, the training data, the lists of numbers, the search by inner product step by step, cost
and value), `spotify.tex` (3 frames) and `airbnb.tex` (3 frames: the first stage of search, two networks and the
nearest homes, three A/B tests; arXiv preprint of January 2026 and Airbnb's blog of April 2025). He asked that
day for five 10-minute cases, one after each Friday block, none older than five years. Which deck goes after
which block, and whether they replace `cases.tex`, is still open (README.md, section "One deck per case").
The same day he found them "too complicated" and asked for them "25% simpler": the words on the slides went down
by 25%, 31% and 36% (README.md, subsection "Simplified"). Keep them at that level of detail.

## Gotchas

- **Every case is a result published in 2021 or later** (Carlos, 2026-11-04). Check the year of every source
  of a card, a map row or a source line before proposing it; an older paper may give background in the Say only.
- **`../style/tools/check.sh` rejects the word "candidate"** in the PDF, among its build-metadata words
  (README.md, CLAUDE.md, TBD, placeholder, FIXME, mockup, .tex), so a slide about retrieval candidates fails the
  check. The slides say "shortlist".
- **The toy numbers of `etsy.tex` (frames 3 and 4) are invented and generated.** `tools/cases_numbers.py` holds
  the search's list and the four items' lists, three numbers each, computes the products, the sums and the order
  (macros `\etQa` to `\etQc`, `\etNa`, `\etNpa`, `\etNsum`, `\etRankAname` and so on) and asserts that the
  necklace comes first and the gift wrap falls out of the top two. Change them there, never in the deck.
- **`\ncell` tints a number by its sign**, as the video store does (`w2-cx-private`, `01_nearest_neighbours`,
  NOTATION.md): blue above 0, orange below, grey for 0, the stronger tint from 2 and from -2 on. It takes an
  integer or a macro that expands to one, and prints a true minus sign.
- **A chip grows with its text.** `stage` (the steps of a search pipeline) has a minimum width of 54 mm; a longer
  label widens one chip only, so give all chips of a pipeline the same `minimum width` (Airbnb frame 1: 59 mm). An arrow label between two
  such chips (`elab`, filled with paper) cuts the top border of the lower chip when the gap is under about 5.5 mm;
  give it `inner sep=0.4pt`, as both funnels of frame 1 do.
- **Every frame is a picture in mm** (`x=1mm, y=1mm`, bounding box `(0,1) rectangle (130,-54)`), as in the
  opener. Body text at `\normalsize` holds about 40 characters a line in the 68 mm left column of a card and
  about 31 in the 53 mm right one.
- **A case card is a two by two grid in the same places on every case frame**: what was built (top left, with
  the box naming this weekend's session), the result (top right), what it cost (bottom left), what was decided
  (bottom right). The `\card...` macros of `cases-kit.tex` fix the geometry, so a new case only fills them.
- **The cells are small.** Top left and both bottom cells: three lines at most (about 110 characters on the
  left, 85 on the right). What a number counts (`\cardnumber`, third argument): two lines, about 36
  characters. A longer text runs into the rule below it; render the frame and look.
- **`tools/check_numbers.py` reads the PDF twice**, in layout mode and in reading order, because a phrase
  that wraps inside one column is cut by the other column in layout mode.
- **To change a number, change `tools/cases_numbers.py`** and rerun it, then rebuild, then rerun
  `tools/check_numbers.py` and `tools/split_parts.py`.
- **A new frame changes the parts.** `tools/split_parts.py` fails when the deck's frame count differs from its
  `PARTS` table; update the table, README.md and this file together.
- **Check the claims against the sources, as well as the numbers.** `tools/check_numbers.py` only proves that
  the numbers reached the PDF. The second read of version 1 found no wrong number but several claims stronger
  than their sources: what a count covers, how many were identified, what a test compared ("a better bandit"
  against the one running). Give every new case such a read by a second agent with the source files; README.md,
  section "Second read", has what the read of version 2 changed.
- **The map's rows are tight.** Four rows at y = -10, -21, -32, -43 and the source line at -48.6; the questions
  are `\normalsize` in 45 mm, the chips at x = 67 (`\mapchip` is 7.6 mm high), the cases column at x = 90 and
  40 mm wide, so "also" holds two company names. A third name wraps to a third line and touches the next row.
- **This repository is public and every push to main publishes it on GitHub Pages**, the deck included. The
  course site (AINIT-PAGE) links it only after Carlos approves it.

## Maintenance contract

- This file is the working memory for this folder. After any major change here, meaning a new or removed
  frame or case, a changed number or source, a changed build or check procedure, or a new place where the deck
  is published, UPDATE THIS FILE AND README.md IN THE SAME COMMIT.
- Every subdirectory that holds work of its own carries its own CLAUDE.md, and that file must itself carry
  this same maintenance contract, including this propagation rule. When you create a subdirectory, create its
  CLAUDE.md at the same time.
- Keep it concrete: real file names, real identifiers, real commands. Record the gotchas that cost someone an
  hour. Do not restate what the code already says plainly.
